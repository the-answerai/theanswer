## Key Benchmarks
- Carousel avg engagement: 10% (vs 7% photos, 6% Reels)
- Saves weighted 3x more than likes
- DM shares weighted 3-5x more than likes
- CTAs increase saves +42%, shares +61%
- 70%+ swipe-through = 3-5x non-follower distribution
- Mixed media (images + video slides) = 2.33% engagement (highest)

## Viral Carousel Archetypes for AI/Tech

1. **Tool Stack** — "5 AI tools that replaced my team"
2. **Before/After Code** — "This line saved 40 hours/week"
3. **Hot Take** — "You don't need microservices"
4. **Cost Breakdown** — "This API call costs $1000/month"
5. **Comparison Grid** — "ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini"
6. **Hidden Feature** — "This GitHub feature nobody knows"
7. **Quiz** — "Test your AI IQ" (890 comments example)
8. **Data Story** — "7 stats that will shock you" (1,400 saves)
9. **Case Study** — "How [X] increased ROI 300%"
10. **Mini-Course** — 8-12 slide deep tutorial

## Design Trends 2026
- AI + Human Touch: AI base + manual "breaking" (grain, sketchy lines)
- Controlled chaos: heavy layering, overlapping backgrounds
- Bright saturated colors dominating
- Neon accents on dark backgrounds
- Bold oversized sans-serif headlines
- Minimal text (2-3 lines max per slide)
