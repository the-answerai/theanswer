# Instagram Carousel Strategy Playbook 2026 — AI/Tech/SaaS Niche

## Key Stats
- Carousels deliver **1.9x higher reach** than single images
- **3x more engagement** than other formats
- Average engagement rate: **10%** (vs 7% photos, 6% Reels)
- **23% more likely** to be algorithm-boosted
- For 1M+ accounts: median reach 217,668 views vs 110,500 for Reels

## 2026 Algorithm Changes
- "Views" is now primary metric across all formats
- **Dwell time** heavily weighted in ranking
- **Saves & shares** > likes
- **Second-chance algorithm**: If someone skips, Instagram shows slide 2
- Completion rate = strong ranking signal

## Optimal Structure: 8-12 Slides

### Slide-by-Slide Framework
1. **Hook** (80% of engagement depends on this) — bold claim, number, or question
2-4. **Build Value** — one idea per slide, 1-2 second read time
5-8. **Deep Content** — step-by-step, data viz, code snippets, proof
9-10. **Micro-CTAs** — "Swipe →", "Next: Proof"
Final. **Close** — Triple CTA (Save + Share + DM)

## 7 Proven Frameworks

### 1. Transformation
Problem → Steps → Results → CTA

### 2. Myth-Buster
Common belief → Why wrong → Truth → CTA

### 3. Checklist (High saves)
Title → One item per slide → Save CTA

### 4. Comparison (Tech/SaaS gold)
"A vs B" → Feature-by-feature → Verdict

### 5. Behind-the-Scenes
"How we built X" → Steps → Results → Lessons

### 6. Hot Take (High engagement)
Controversial claim → Evidence → Nuance → "Agree?"

### 7. Hidden Feature
Unknown feature → Problem it solves → How to use → Share CTA

## Hook Formulas

### By Pattern
- **Question**: "Are you making this mistake?"
- **Number**: "5 AI tools that replaced my team"
- **Bold Claim**: "This one change 10x'd our metric"
- **FOMO**: "Before this gets patched..."
- **Pain Point**: "If you've ever [X], swipe"
- **Curiosity Gap**: "Before/After inside →"
- **Hot Take**: "Everyone is wrong about [X]"

### AI/Tech Specific
- "ChatGPT can't do this. But [tool] can."
- "This AI feature is hidden in plain sight"
- "The API call costing you $1000/month"
- "5 AI prompts that actually work"

### Rules
- 5-12 words max
- 48px+ font for headlines
- High contrast
- Directional cues (arrows, "→")

## Design Specs

### Technical
- **Dimensions**: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5)
- **Max slides**: 20 (use 8-12)
- **Font — Headlines**: 60-80px
- **Font — Body**: 24-30px minimum
- **Font — Code**: 20-24px monospace

### Color Schemes for Tech
| Type | Primary | Secondary | Accent |
|------|---------|-----------|--------|
| AI/ML | Deep Blue #0A1929 | Electric Blue #3B82F6 | Neon Green #10B981 |
| Dev Tools | Dark Gray #1F2937 | Purple #8B5CF6 | Cyan #06B6D4 |
| SaaS | Navy #1E3A8A | Coral #F59E0B | White #FFFFFF |
| Data | Charcoal #374151 | Teal #14B8A6 | Orange #F97316 |

### Rules
- 2-3 brand colors max
- High contrast for accessibility
- Test on mobile
- White space > cluttered
- Consistent spacing throughout

## Copy Rules

### Text Density
| Slide Type | Headline | Body | Max Words |
|-----------|----------|------|-----------|
| Hook | 5-12 words | None | 12 |
| Content | 8-15 words | 15-25 words | 40 |
| Data/Stats | 10-20 words | 20-30 words | 50 |
| Code | 5-10 words | Code block | 60 |
| CTA | 10-15 words | 10-20 words | 35 |

### Caption
- First sentence = main keyword
- 125-150 words optimal
- 5-8 niche hashtags

## CTA Strategy (Triple CTA)

### Save CTA
"Save this for the next time you [action]"

### Share CTA
"Send this to someone who handles [task]"

### DM/Comment CTA
"DM 'TEMPLATE' and I'll send the full version"

### Micro-CTAs (throughout slides)
"Swipe →", "Keep swiping", "Next: [teaser]", "Almost there"

## What Drives Engagement

### Saves (algorithm gold)
Templates, scripts, checklists, decision trees, pricing, timelines

### Shares
Hot takes, relatable pain points, surprising insights, comparisons

### Comments
Questions, polls, fill-in-blank, debates, "tag someone"

## Performance Targets
- Swipe-through rate: **60%+**
- Completion rate: **50%+**
- Save rate: **5%+ of reach**
- Share rate: **3%+ of reach**

## Posting Frequency
- **3-5 carousels/week** (prioritize these)
- 2-4 Reels/week (discovery)
- 5-7 Stories/day (engagement)

### Content Mix
- 40% Educational (tutorials, guides)
- 30% Data/Insights (stats, benchmarks)
- 20% Hot Takes (opinions, debates)
- 10% Community (UGC, behind-scenes)

## Common Mistakes
- Weak opening slide (use bold visuals + hooks)
- Too much text per slide (1-2 second read time)
- No visual flow between slides
- Forgetting slide 2 matters (second-chance algorithm)
- Missing or unclear CTA
- Generic advice instead of specific data
- Not testing on mobile
- Ignoring saves/shares in favor of likes
