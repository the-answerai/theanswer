## Key Features Deep Dive

### 1. Agent Teams (Most Discussed)
- Multiple agents work in parallel
- Coordinate autonomously
- Split complex tasks into segmented jobs
- Research preview in Claude Code

### 2. 1 Million Token Context Window
- Can process 1,500 pages of text
- 30,000+ lines of code
- Over 1 hour of video content
- Previous limit: 200,000 tokens

### 3. 500 Zero-Day Vulnerabilities Found
- Used only out-of-the-box capabilities
- All validated by Anthropic team or external researchers
- Found flaws in well-tested codebases undetected for decades

### 4. Office Integration
- **Claude in Excel**: Pivot tables, charts, conditional formatting, finance-grade formatting
- **Claude in PowerPoint** (Research preview): Reads layouts, fonts, slide masters; stays on brand

### 5. "Vibe Working" Concept
Scott White (Head of Product for Enterprise):
> "I think that we are now transitioning almost into vibe working... users can hand over broad goals rather than precise instructions, trusting the system to plan, execute, and iterate."
